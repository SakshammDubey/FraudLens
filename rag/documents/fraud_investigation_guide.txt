Fraud Investigation Reference Guide

PURPOSE
A high fraud-risk score is an indicator for further review,
not proof that a transaction is fraudulent.

TRANSACTION AMOUNT
An unusual transaction amount may warrant investigation
when compared with an established customer baseline.
The amount alone is insufficient to establish fraud.

TRANSACTION PATTERNS
Repeated transactions, unusual transaction timing, or
unexpected changes in transaction behaviour may warrant
additional checks when reliable supporting data is available.

IDENTITY AND DEVICE INFORMATION
Device, browser, and identity-related attributes may provide
additional context when available. Missing identity data
must not be treated as evidence of fraud.

INVESTIGATION PROCESS
1. Review the model's predicted risk and relevant features.
2. Compare available evidence with documented fraud patterns.
3. Identify missing information and alternative explanations.
4. Recommend appropriate verification steps.
5. Do not conclude fraud without sufficient evidence.
